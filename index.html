DoCW Official Rulebook

Treasure Gamble

An 8-round relay that tests your team's physical and mental agility, and your risk management. Success takes flawless communication between base and field — balancing high-value tasks against the clock.

Team Roles
IM

The Inside Member

Control Room

Stays isolated at base for the whole event. Draws tasks from the difficulty bowl and relays instructions to the Outside Team. While the team runs the course, the Inside Member gets exclusive access to mini-games — a chance to gamble the team's current points for a shot at multiplying their score.

FN

The Outside Team

Field Navigators

Navigate across campus to each waypoint and execute whatever physical or mental challenge the Inside Member calls out. Every task must be performed in front of a designated DoCW core member on-site to validate completion and earn points.

The 8-Round Cycle
1

Draw & Relay

The Inside Member selects a task difficulty — Easy, Medium, or Hard — from the bowl, then communicates the specific challenge to the team waiting at their current location.

2

Execute or Bail

The Outside Team attempts the task. If it looks too difficult or time-consuming, they can strategically skip it. Skipping saves time, but incurs a strict point penalty.

3

The Clue

Once the task is completed (or officially skipped), the on-site core member hands over a clue directing the team to their next hidden location.

4

Reset & Repeat

On arrival at the new location, the Outside Team relays the news back to the Inside Member, triggering the next draw.

This cycle repeats exactly 8 times.
